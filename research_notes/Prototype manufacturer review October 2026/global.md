# China and US PCB/PCBA manufacturers: six engineering samples

Research accessed 2026-10-15. Scope supplied by coordinator: A 30 × 34 mm, B 30 × 51 mm, one common electrical BOM, two layouts/CPLs, four layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, ENIG, nominal 35 µm copper on every layer, 46 fitted SMT placements plus connector shield soldering; three assembled samples of each. Current routing is incomplete, so these are sourcing findings, not an approved manufacturing package. No vendor was contacted, no design uploaded, no account created, and no order or binding quote obtained. All money below is USD before unquoted taxes, import handling and shipping unless stated otherwise.

## Which manufacturers can supply three A and three B samples, and at what published price?

### Takeaway

PCBWay, Seeed Fusion and Elecrow are credible turnkey candidates for this small build, subject to component and assembly review. JLCPCB is a useful cost benchmark but exact three-piece assembly selection and Economic eligibility need confirmation. ALLPCB is a quote candidate with less definite public small-quantity terms. OSH Park is a transparent bare-board alternative, not a turnkey six-PCBA supplier.

## Does combining A and B or ordering more units save money?

### Takeaway

Pooling purchasing and one shipment are low-risk savings opportunities. A mixed A+B fabrication/assembly panel can save repeated setup and feeder charges, but it is still two designs, may move the order into a more expensive assembly tier, and wastes extra board area. At six samples, compare two jobs in one shipment against one explicitly approved mixed panel; do not assume the panel is cheaper.

### Cited Findings

- JLCPCB explicitly regards separable boards with different traces or silkscreen as different designs, even if supplied in one Gerber. Different designs attract an additional PCB fabrication charge whose value was not established here. [Different-design policy](https://jlcpcb.com/quote/pcbOrderFaq/Different%20Design%20in%20Panel).
- JLCPCB's own October 2026 worked example found a 2×1 V-cut Standard panel $12.89 **more** than Economic singles at ten boards, and $14.87 less at fifty; its design is unrelated to this one, so this is evidence of crossover behavior, not our quote. [Vendor worked example](https://jlcpcb.com/blog/reduce-pcb-assembly-cost-prototype).
- Economic assembly supports mouse-bite panels; V-cut is Standard. The advertised bulk per-board assembly minimum is irrelevant to a six-sample prototype. [Panel FAQ](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-faqs), [panel pricing explanation](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/panelizing-your-pcb-for-assembly).
- Elecrow's panel PDF dated 2018 says identical-design panelization free and each extra different design $12. Its older 2017 blog instead charges identical-design panel fees. Treat $12 only as historical indicative policy; current fee needs confirmation. [2018 PDF](https://www.elecrow.com/download/How-to-Reduce-Cost-with-Panelizing-Service.pdf), [conflicting 2017 blog](https://www.elecrow.com/blog/how-to-reduce-cost-with-panelizing-service.html).
- OSH Park panel guidelines require 2.54 mm between outlines, and a 5.08 mm-wide frame to guarantee intact arrival. Quantity applies to panels, so one A+B panel ordered through prototype service yields three A+B pairs. [Panel requirements](https://docs.oshpark.com/troubleshooting/panelized-designs/).

### Inferences

- **JLC assembly-only algebra, conditional, not a quote:** let E be chargeable Extended types and U all unique fitted types. Two Economic jobs have setup/stencil/feeder subtotal `19.42 + 6.14E`; one eligible mixed-panel job `17.92 + 3.07E`, saving `1.50 + 3.07E` before panel fabrication/handling changes. E=10 gives $32.20 and E=20 gives $62.90 savings. These E values are sensitivity examples, not verified BOM classifications. Source rates: [current price table](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price).
- **Standard one-sided algebra:** two jobs `67.54 + 3.06U`; combined `41.98 + 1.53U`; difference `25.56 + 1.53U`, before fixture/rail/PCB/inspection changes. Components, placement labor and solder joints remain needed for all six boards. Actual quote may program each subdesign separately rather than grant these maximum shared-setup savings. Source rates: [price table](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price).
- **OSH Park area counterexample:** the two separate bounding rectangles sum to 2,550 mm² and cost $39.53 per three-of-each. A conservative side-by-side mixed panel with 2.54 mm gap and the specified 5.08 mm frame is `(30+2.54+30+10.16) × (51+10.16) = 72.70 × 61.16 mm`, yielding about $68.92 at the same published per-set rate. It is approximately $29.39 dearer before separate assembly. This is illustrative geometry, not an accepted panel CAM quote; nesting B's empty corners could change area. [Price rate](https://docs.oshpark.com/services/four-layer/), [panel constraints](https://docs.oshpark.com/troubleshooting/panelized-designs/).
- For three versus five assembled per design, compare **total cash**, not unit price. Four additional fitted BOMs and potentially more X-ray/tests can exceed setup dilution savings; purchasing ten modules can cross its observed price tier but does not automatically justify four extra full boards. [Module tier](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/EspressifSystems-ESP32_C6_MINI_1N4/C5736265).
- Shared procurement does not require mixed copper: request two PCB/CPL jobs under one commercial order, one common exact MPN purchase pool/attrition pool, and one shipment. Savings require vendor agreement; no public promise that feeder or engineering charges are pooled across independent jobs was found.
- Do not cut A and B from a single universal electrical board: it would alter geometry, thermal behavior and the controlled experiment. Mixed panels must retain each approved outline, no ties to B neck/island and no copper/rail in either antenna exclusion.

### Gaps

- Current mixed-design fabrication surcharges, whether one panel reuses machine programming/feeders, and paid depanelization/casework are not publicly quantified for these exact layouts.
- Courier cost for two combined jobs versus separate parcels needs final packed weight, ship-to postcode, declared value and shipping terms. No arbitrary shipping saving has been assumed.

## What factors beyond headline price should determine the choice?

### Takeaway

Select the vendor that accepts the exact stackup, sensor process, constrained depanelization, controlled component identities and inspection/test instructions in writing. Low-cost pooled manufacturing can change copper, reflow, spare-board condition and review service; those differences matter directly to thermal accuracy and prototype yield.

### Cited Findings

- JLC Economic is limited to standard stackups, single-sided placement, minimum 0402 and 0.4 mm IC pitch, fixed 255±5 °C reflow and no SPI; Standard supports custom stackups, double-side and SPI with nominal 240±5 °C. Standard minimum single/panel dimensions are 70×70 mm, so these 30 mm boards require an approved panel/rails if Standard. Published assembly times are 1–3 days Economic and ≥4 Standard, not sourcing-plus-fabrication-plus-delivery. [Capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities).
- JLC assembly terms require component body at least 2.5 mm from PCB edge, describe automatic tooling/fiducial modifications, and warn spare unassembled Economic boards may nevertheless pass through paste/reflow. These are significant exceptions to discuss for the USB connector, sensor island and restricted outline. [Assembly terms](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/terms-and-conditions-of-jlcpcb-assembly-service).
- JLC's prototype page says out-of-stock global sourcing can take 9–15 days; no guaranteed date follows from a stocked ESP32 alone. [Sourcing time](https://jlcpcb.com/solutions/pcb-prototype-assembly).
- PCBWay accepts turnkey, kitted and hybrid sourcing, including specified suppliers; supports 0402 and leadless packages. Odd outlines generally need arrays and break-away rails. Its assembly clock starts only after all parts/files are ready; optional programming/testing needs methods supplied. [Capabilities](https://www.pcbway.com/assembly-capabilities.html), [supplier choice/quantity Q&A](https://www.pcbway.com/blog/PCB_Basic_Information/PCBWay_Q_A_003___Common_Questions_for_PCBA_Ordering_01.html), [assembly services](https://www.pcbway.com/pcb-assembly.html).
- Seeed advertises engineering DFA review, turnkey SMT/THT, functional testing options, no MOQ, and free shipping on PCBA orders. Its seven-working-day claim is conditional on **all** parts being in OPL; quoted stock/lead time and delivery terms for the exact filtered sensor still required. [Service conditions](https://www.seeedstudio.com/pcb-assembly.html).
- Elecrow's current service lists lead-free assembly, 0402/QFN, SPI/AOI/ICT and functional testing options, dedicated project manager and general three-week lead time. Testing/flashing and jig effort are separate quote items, and sourced components can come from named distributors or the local market; specify authorized-distributor-only sourcing for critical parts. [Service](https://static-cdn.elecrow.com/pcb-assembly.html).
- ALLPCB supports turnkey, consigned and partial-turnkey SMT/THT, 0.25 mm fine-pitch, box build and inspection services. Its quote form says customs/VAT excluded and engineering review may alter final price; request a line-item test/inspection quote, not generic quality marketing. [Capabilities](https://www.allpcb.com/pcba.html), [quote conditions](https://www.allpcb.com/pcba_quote.html).
- OSH Park's actual four-layer stackup uses approximately 17.5 µm (0.5 oz) **inner** copper, not the project's assumed 35 µm all layers. It uses FR408HR and ENIG and publishes detailed drill/routing capabilities. Adopting it needs explicit stackup/thermal-model review, not a price-only substitution. It advertises free worldwide shipping; this does not state import taxes or guaranteed transit. [Stackup](https://docs.oshpark.com/services/four-layer/), [shipping claim](https://docs.oshpark.com/services/).
- ALLPCB's new-user $1 promotion only discounts PCB fabrication (base ≤$30 → $1; otherwise $30 off) plus up to $30 first-shipment discount, and cannot stack with other coupons. Account eligibility and actual shipment remain conditions; not a recurring production price. [Promotion conditions](https://www.allpcb.com/Activity/NewMemberActivityAd).

### Inferences

- For **this** prototype, priority order should be: exact procurement and protected sensor handling; true layer copper/stackup; mechanically safe tab positions and shipping protection; documented hidden-pad solder inspection; no unauthorized CPL/MPN changes; then total landed cash and date.
- Sensirion handling must prohibit unapproved cleaning/coating/adhesive contamination near the sensor opening, require correct filter suffix, and reconcile the vendor's reflow profile with the sensor manufacturer's process. Peak 255±5 °C alone is not proof of incompatibility; time above liquidus, complete thermal profile and the filtered AD1F limits require comparison. This report has not independently approved or rejected any vendor's profile for the exact sensor.
- Request exposed-pad/QFN/module-ground solder verification with saved first-article images, X-ray scope and acceptance criteria. AOI alone cannot prove all hidden ground joints. Functional low-power/USB/sensor testing is a separately priced service, not implied by factory workmanship inspection.
- Pack cells locally for first bring-up if practical: quote bare assembled electronics plus enclosure prints separately from batteries/harness. This reduces shipping ambiguity; battery pack acceptance and protected VBAT simulator procedure still govern first power-on.
- Six assembled boards are only one deliverable layer. Batteries, matched keyed harnesses, six enclosure sets, labels, firmware programming, fixture, measurement procedure and acceptance records must be explicitly included or excluded when comparing totals.

### Gaps

- No vendor has accepted this specific narrow B neck, RF keepouts, sensor process, current uncompleted routing, exact .127 mm/annular rules, copper on every layer, CPL rotations, or forbidden tab positions.
- No formal assurance of three good samples per variant, sensor contamination controls, current component traceability records, test coverage, enclosure tolerances, or replacement/rework rights has been obtained.
- Final country-specific import/VAT treatment and battery-carriage rules should come from official customs/carrier sources in the coordinator's landed-cost section; generic vendor free-shipping marketing does not settle them.
