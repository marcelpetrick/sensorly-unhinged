# Choose prototype manufacturers by accepted scope

**Recommendation: request comparable 3 A + 3 B assembly quotations from AISLER and Eurocircuits, with PCBWay and JLCPCB as the overseas cost comparison.** Beta LAYOUT, LeitOn, Seeed Fusion and Elecrow provide useful alternatives. AISLER's published four-layer formula gives **€37.95 net, approximately €45.16 including 19% VAT, for six bare boards**; OSH Park's rate gives **$39.53 before import charges**, with a different inner-copper stackup. Neither number buys six populated instruments. No manufacturer-specific, delivered PCBA quotation was obtained: exact parts, panel support, inspection, freight and test scope still require project review. Consolidating procurement and shipment is the first saving to seek; a mixed A+B panel saves money only when avoided setup exceeds its extra area, engineering and handling. The present design remains on manufacturing hold, so use this review to prepare quotations rather than place an order.

Research date: **15 October 2026**. Scope: Germany delivery, three accepted assembled boards of each physical variant. Evidence is public manufacturer/distributor material accessed on that date, repository sources and explicitly identified calculations or assumptions. No vendor was contacted, files uploaded, account created or order placed. Published prices and observed stock are neither binding quotations nor reservations.

## Six assembled boards require two controlled layouts

At repository checkpoint `23a7fd6`, A is **30 × 34 mm** and B has a **30 × 51 mm bounding rectangle**. B therefore does **not** fit a 50 × 50 mm envelope. Its actual routed material area is 1,210 mm², including the 3.5 × 8 mm neck and 18 × 9 mm island; billing must follow the manufacturer's area rules rather than assume that cutouts reduce price. The intended platform is four-layer, approximately 1.6 mm FR-4, ENIG, with nominal 35 µm copper on every layer; the selected supplier's actual stackup must be reviewed. Both variants share one electrical BOM but have separate placement files, including a different C6 rotation. There are **46 fitted placements, 26 unique fitted MPNs and R21 DNP**. The fitted-package count used here is 198 SMT plus four USB-shield through-hole joints per board. These are repository-derived inputs from [the shared model](tools/boardgen/design.py), [geometry](tools/boardgen/variants.py) and [BOM](hardware/outputs/bom.csv).

Both boards have zero recorded DRC errors, warnings and schematic parity differences, but **A still has 59 and B 52 unconnected items**. Current outputs are drafts. The [execution plan](docs/01-execution-plan.md) also requires pack/harness evidence, assembler stackup/DFM/CPL approval and a named prototype-order review. A sourcing report cannot close those gates. Three of each is a smaller engineering lot than the original **5 A + 5 B** experiment; it does not automatically satisfy that plan's sample-count or measurement gates.

Compare three distinct deliverables. A **bare PCB** includes fabrication, not components or soldering. A **PCBA** includes agreed parts and assembly, but not automatically firmware, functional tests or batteries. A **complete sample** additionally needs a qualified pack/NTC/harness, insulation, enclosure, labels, flashing and serialized acceptance records. Ask for **three accepted, depanelized units per variant**, and define the remedy if fewer pass the agreed test; ordering three placements alone does not guarantee three working samples.

## Procurement pooling is safer than assuming panel savings

There are **four different economies**, and they should be quoted separately. **One procurement pool and one parcel** can reduce repeated purchasing, allowances or freight while retaining two PCB jobs. JLC explicitly offers combined shipping with 15 days' free warehousing, but final weight and order/coupon restrictions affect savings. **Vendor production pooling** combines unrelated work at the factory and is already built into many published prototype rates; it does not mean our two layouts share an assembly program. **An explicit A+B customer panel** can reduce repeated machine setup but adds area, tooling and depanelization. **Overbuying five or six of each** spreads fixed costs across more units but normally increases total cash. [Combined shipping](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/combine-shipping-service), [order additions](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-add-a-new-order-to-your-existing-order), [coupon rules](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-do-i-use-a-coupon-for-my-order).

An identical BOM helps feeder and procurement reuse, but **two CPLs, stencil geometries, board origins and rotation reviews remain**. JLC treats separable differing layouts as different designs and can charge accordingly. AISLER explicitly excludes mixed-design panels in its standard production-panel policy. Eurocircuits supports mixed-circuit fabrication panels, but that does not promise a single assembly setup price. Beta requires separate orders for assembly variants, at least 10 mm spacing on customer assembly panels, and separate labeled shared-component supplies for separate projects. None provides evidence that merely merging files removes a job charge. [JLC design policy](https://jlcpcb.com/quote/pcbOrderFaq/Different%20Design%20in%20Panel), [AISLER panel policy](https://community.aisler.net/t/aisler-production-panel-specification/2779), [Eurocircuits panel guide](https://www.eurocircuits.com/user-guides/visualizer-user-guides/panel-editor-user-guide/), [Beta assembly guide](https://eu.beta-layout.com/pcb/assembly/assembly-guide/).

Panel geometry can erase savings. An illustrative OSH Park side-by-side pair, allowing its specified 2.54 mm outline gap and 5.08 mm perimeter frame, occupies **72.70 × 61.16 mm**. At its published rate three paired panels cost **$68.92**, approximately $29.39 more than separate three-copy sets. A narrower stacked illustration, **40.16 × 97.70 mm**, calculates to **$60.82** and still costs more. Neither is optimized, CAM-approved or proof that a better interlocked nest is impossible; both demonstrate why a shared panel is not automatically cheaper. Tab land, internal supports and sensor protection still need design work. [OSH panel requirements](https://docs.oshpark.com/troubleshooting/panelized-designs/).

Both boards are small and B is irregular. PCBWay requests arrays/rails for small or odd boards; straight V-score cannot define the B outline. The panel drawing must keep tabs off the **B neck, sensor island and approximately top 6 mm antenna strip**, and locate supports/depanelization loads on the electronics body. Require depanelized delivery and a reviewed separation method. A minimum five paired bare panels yields five A plus five B bare boards: ask whether three panels can be assembled and two returned unpopulated, without treating bare MOQ as assembly MOQ. [PCBWay assembly reminders](https://www.pcbway.com/assembly-special-remind.html), [panel requirements](https://www.pcbway.com/pcb_prototype/Panel_Requirements_for_Assembly.html).

The conditional JLC fee model makes the maximum opportunity visible. Its published table gives Economic setup $8.18, stencil $1.53, Extended feeder $3.07/type and manual-work labor $3.58/order; Standard one-sided setup/stencil are $25.56/$8.21 with $1.53/type feeder loading. Mixed-design panel fee is $8.21; placement is $0.0016/SMT joint and $0.0164/manual joint. Inspection, fixtures and actual eligibility remain additional. [Current fee table](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price).

Let **E** be the actual chargeable Extended type count, not an assumed nine. Including manual-order labor, one Economic job's fixed allowance is `F = 13.29 + 3.07E` dollars. Two jobs versus one eligible shared job plus the mixed-panel fee would save **`F − 8.21 = 5.08 + 3.07E` dollars**, before extra fabrication, panel engineering and inspection. Illustrative sensitivities E = 9 and E = 26 give **$32.71 and $84.90** respectively; these are not verified bounds on this BOM's chargeable count. Excluding manual-order labor from both cases gives `1.50 + 3.07E`; that narrower formula is not a conflicting price. Neither formula establishes that this board qualifies for Economic or that the manufacturer shares all setup. A switch to Standard can remove the apparent saving.

JLC Economic permits **standard stackups and single-sided assembly only**. Standard supports custom stackups, but its published minimum board/panel size is **70 × 70 mm**. Both individual layouts are smaller, so Standard requires an accepted panel/rail solution with additional area, support and handling costs. Determine the eligible tier before comparing setup algebra. [JLC assembly capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities).

For quantity increases, AISLER's bare 6 + 6 calculation costs **€9.95 more cash** than 3 + 3 while lowering average bare cost from €6.32 to €3.99. Four more populated boards additionally need four fitted BOMs and testing. The simple six-board joint allowance is **$2.2944**; four extra boards add about $1.53 of that placement/manual-joint allowance, but parts dominate. At the assumed €12–18 fitted BOM below, those four boards add **€48–72 in parts alone**. Request 3 + 3 and 5 + 5 options; choose extra units for spares and experimental value, rather than label a lower unit price a lower project spend.

Do not apply the old blanket “below €150 means duty-free” rule. Current Commission guidance describes a **€3 charge per tariff-item category for qualifying low-value B2C distance-sale consignments from 1 July 2026**, with stated scope/exceptions. It is not €3 per PCB and does not settle B2B industrial procurement or consignments above €150. Keep duty **D** and carrier/clearance charges **K** variable until classification, origin, customer status and Incoterms are confirmed. Germany's normal import-VAT rate is 19%; a non-IOSS consumer model is approximately `(goods + freight + D) × 1.19 + K`, with K's tax treatment according to its invoice. IOSS collects VAT at sale: do not add import VAT again. VAT-registered eligible businesses should compare costs excluding recoverable VAT, while tracking cash paid and documentation. Request an itemized landed/DDP offer. ## The winning quote must preserve the instrument and experiment

**Manufacturing process is part of measurement validity.** Build A and B with the same agreed laminate, thickness, copper weights, finish and preferably common material/component lots, retaining records for the later 20-unit series. Do not compare an A fabricated with one stackup against a B from another. Specify exact MPNs, the filtered sensor suffix, R21 DNP/paste treatment, pin-1/polarity review and both CPL previews. A bare electrical net test, visual inspection, AOI, SPI, X-ray and a powered functional test cover different failure classes. Require hidden-joint inspection scope for the module ground lands, charger/regulator and sensor, and explicit USB-shield through-hole soldering rather than assuming SMT placement completes the board.

Sensirion's handling instructions require avoiding washing, unsuitable chemicals/VOCs and contamination, appropriate ESD protection, and reviewed solder handling; filtered devices still need correct processing. Multiple-cycle assembly and post-reflow conditioning can affect RH behavior. Agree no unreviewed coating, adhesive, cleaning or packaging near the aperture, and compare the **complete** thermal profile against manufacturer limits. JLC publishes Economic nominal 255 ± 5 °C versus Standard 240 ± 5 °C, while Beta describes vapour-phase soldering and warns that some sensors require compatibility confirmation. A nominal peak alone does **not** prove that either process is incompatible; obtain a manufacturer-accepted profile and handling disposition for this exact sensor. [Sensirion handling](https://sensirion.com/media/documents/6D95AA80/6840311F/HT_Handling_Instructions_SHTxx.pdf), [sensor specification](https://sensirion.com/media/documents/33FD6951/67EB9032/HT_DS_Datasheet_SHT4x_5.pdf), [JLC process capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities), [Beta process guide](https://eu.beta-layout.com/pcb/assembly/assembly-guide/).

Inspection inclusions differ. AISLER's assembly portfolio lists manual optical inspection, with AOI on request; Eurocircuits lists visual/SPI/X-ray. JLC distinguishes Economic without SPI from Standard with SPI, and its terms include edge-clearance requirements and possible tooling modifications that need exceptions for this outline. Require saved first-article images and named acceptance criteria rather than infer quality from country or a generic “tested” label. Programming, safe first power-on, low-current measurements and sensor/RF behavior require supplied procedures and separately agreed scope. [AISLER portfolio](https://community.aisler.net/t/assembly-portfolio/4674), [Eurocircuits inspection scope](https://www.eurocircuits.com/services/pcb-assembly-capabilities/), [JLC assembly terms](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/terms-and-conditions-of-jlcpcb-assembly-service).

Component logistics can outweigh nominal assembly cost. AISLER charges customer-supplied parts per unique MPN per project; Eurocircuits requires feeder-compatible tape and surplus, while Beta publishes minimum overage. Obtain an actual turnkey allowance or consignment list before buying six of every item. Ask who owns and returns unused stock, whether excess bare boards see paste/reflow, and whether stored inventory/stencils/programs can be reused for 20 units. Compare replacement/rework terms, complaint windows, return freight, named engineering support and end-to-end delivery dates, not only the number of assembly days. [AISLER supplied parts](https://community.aisler.net/t/customer-supplied-parts-for-your-assembly-project/1411), [Eurocircuits feeding](https://www.eurocircuits.com/technical-guidelines/assembly-manufacturing-technology/pick-place/), [Beta overage](https://eu.beta-layout.com/pcb/assembly/general-information/).
